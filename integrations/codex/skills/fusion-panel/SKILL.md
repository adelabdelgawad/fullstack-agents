# Independent perspectives

Keep the question and evidence bounded. When delegation is authorized, use
independent Codex reviewers with explicit lenses and path lists; otherwise
take sequential passes yourself. Do not hardcode model names or spawn Claude
planning agents. The Codex lead verifies source claims and resolves conflicts.
Summarize the recommendation, evidence, contradictions and uncertainty. Use
Claude's bounded audit handoff for the resulting implementation, not for every
reasoning step. Routine tasks do not need a panel.
